Redan 1597 fanns skvaltkvarnar här, och genom historien har Håfors huserat många verksamheter. På Idala-sidan fanns 3 gårdar, 3 kvarnar, elverk, såg, snickeri och Ragnhilds affär. På Gällinge-sidan fanns 4 gårdar, 2 kvarnar, mejeri, smedja, postkontor, telefonstation och skola.

Exempelvis: Johannes kvarn (från 1882), Annersa kvarn och Håfors mejeri (1896–1940).

## Håfors – A Village Between Two Parishes

The village of Håfors is split by the border between Gällinge and Idala parishes. Here, the river Löftaån created a powerful waterfall which powered mills from as early as 1597.

The Idala side included farms, mills, a power plant, sawmill, carpentry workshop, and a shop. The Gällinge side had farms, mills, a dairy, forge, post office, telephone exchange, and a school.

Examples: Johannes Mill (1882), Annersa Mill, and Håfors Dairy (1896–1940).
